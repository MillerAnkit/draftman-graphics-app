Step — Before we start

Payment terms & house policy

Payment schedule

60% of the total cost of the service(s) booked is due before work begins. The remaining 40% balance is due in full before final files are delivered.

House policy

  • Work begins only after the 60% deposit is confirmed.
  • Final, high-resolution files are released only after the balance is paid in full.
  • Revisions follow the number agreed at quoting — extra revisions may attract a fee.
  • Deadlines depend on how complete and prompt your input/feedback is.
  • Deposits are non-refundable once work has started; cancellations before work starts may be refunded minus a processing fee.
  • All information you share is used only to deliver your project and is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • Ownership/usage rights transfer to you upon full and final payment.
